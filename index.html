Historical Background & Academic Success



Government Graduate College Okara is one of the oldest and most respected educational institutions in the region, known for its strong academic tradition and commitment to quality education. Since its establishment, the college has played a vital role in educating thousands of students who have gone on to succeed in various professional fields. With experienced faculty, disciplined learning environments, and consistent academic performance, the college maintains an impressive success rate in university examinations. Over the years, it has built a reputation for producing capable graduates who contribute positively to society and the nation's development.

Financial Aid & Student Support



At Government Graduate College Okara, financial assistance programs are available to help talented and deserving students continue their education without financial barriers. The college facilitates government initiatives such as the CM Honhaar Scholarship Programme Punjab, which provides financial support to high-achieving students from deserving backgrounds. In addition, students benefit from the CM Punjab Laptop Scheme, through which modern laptops are distributed to promote digital learning and research. These programs reflect the commitment of Maryam Nawaz's provincial government to empower youth with both financial support and technological resources, enabling them to excel in their academic and professional journeys.

Events at GC Okara.

Students of Government Graduate College Okara won a bronze medal at the Punjab-level Anti-Corruption competitions held on International Anti-Corruption Day 2025. The event was organized by the National Accountability Bureau Lahore. Principal Ali Haider Dogar appreciated the students and faculty for this proud achievement. 🥉🎓

Students of Government Graduate College Okara alumna Azka Amin achieved success in the Provincial Management Service (PMS) Examination. Principal Ali Haider Dogar congratulated her on this proud achievement and appreciated her dedication and hard work, which brought honor to the college and inspired current students. 🎓🏅

Today the college community of Government Graduate College Okara honored Prof. Riaz Khan for his nearly 30 years of dedicated service to the institution. An Iftar cum Farewell gathering was arranged where the Principal and staff appreciated his professionalism, graceful hosting, and memorable contributions to countless college events. The college family expressed gratitude for his remarkable service and wished him good health, happiness, and success in the new chapter of his life. 🌟🎤🌹

The Rescue 1122 team visited Government Graduate College Okara to conduct an informative session on emergency preparedness and life-saving techniques. Students actively participated in practical demonstrations and learned important skills such as first aid, disaster management, and quick emergency response. The college appreciated the efforts of the Rescue 1122 team for spreading awareness and equipping students with valuable safety knowledge. 🚨💪

An awareness session was conducted at Government Graduate College Okara by Civil Defense Okara to educate students about fire prevention and emergency response techniques. Officers demonstrated practical methods to handle fire incidents and other emergency situations. Students and teachers were also encouraged to register as Civil Defense volunteers, promoting community safety and preparedness. 🔥👏

A Parents_Teachers Meeting is currently being held at Government Graduate College Okara. Students are requested to visit the college along with their parents to receive their result cards and examination papers from their teachers. 📚👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

A Welcome Party was organized at Government Graduate College Okara to warmly receive the 1st year students. The event was filled with excitement, performances, and positive energy as senior students and faculty welcomed the newcomers, encouraging them to begin their academic journey with confidence and enthusiasm. 🎉🎓

Principal's Message


At Government Graduate College Okara, our mission is to provide quality education in a supportive and disciplined environment. As an institution affiliated with University of the Punjab, we offer diverse academic programs guided by experienced faculty, well-equipped laboratories, and excellent sports facilities that help students grow academically and personally. Through our admission campaign, we invite talented students to join this prestigious college. The Government of Punjab also supports students through initiatives such as the CM Honhaar Scholarship Programme Punjab and the CM Punjab Laptop Scheme, ensuring opportunities for both financial support and digital learning. Prof. Ali Haider Dogar Principal
